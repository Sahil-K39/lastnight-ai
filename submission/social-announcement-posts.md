# Social Announcement Posts

## LinkedIn Post

```text
I built LastNight AI for the OpenAI x Outskill AI Builders Hackathon.

LastNight AI is an AI exam survival agent for students who start studying with hours left before an exam. Students can upload syllabus notes, PYQs, lecture files, or handwritten material, choose how much time is left, and get a structured study kit with:

• Exam strategy
• PYQ pattern analysis
• Unit-wise importance
• High-priority topics
• Important questions
• Revision notes
• Viva questions
• Quiz questions
• Panic-mode study plan

This hackathon was not a pitch competition. It was a shipping competition, so I focused on building a working product.

Tech stack:
React, Vite, TypeScript, Express, Three.js, Flutter, Gemini Flash, and Ollama.

How I used Codex:
Codex helped me review the project, fix bugs, improve the UI, add cinematic 3D design, sync the web and Flutter app, add login/profile/PYQ upload flows, replace paid OpenAI API usage with Gemini/Ollama provider modes, harden secrets, prepare GitHub, and create final submission assets.

GitHub:
https://github.com/Sahil-K39/lastnight-ai

I would love feedback on the product idea, demo flow, and what students would need most during last-night exam preparation.

#AIBuildersHackathon #Outskill #OpenAI #Codex
```
